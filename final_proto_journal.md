# Final Prototype Reflective journal

## Final Prototype week 1

The time has come to work on out final prototypes! For this final project, I wondered if there was anything that I had wanted to make this semester that I did not get to make for whatever reason, and after reading my past journal entries I remembered that I had wanted to make a sort of mold growth simulation but had given up on the idea because we had not went over arrays yet. This is what inspired the idea of creating a kind of micro world simulation, as I have always loved coding things that have behaviors that simulate life in a way. I have made an [ecosystem simulation](https://www.slnova.org/yogurtfairy/projects/934296/play/) in Starlogo Nova before, and a [virtual petri dish that reacts to light, tilting and shaking](https://github.com/yogurtdairy/CART253/blob/main/images/VID_20260402_173738.mp4) in Max msp, but I have never made anything of this kind before using JavaScript. I love biology (it was actually one of my strongest subjects in high school, I even got an excellence award), and despite not choosing to pursue this subject in my higher education, I still enjoy incorporating biological systems and themes into my work like natural ecosystems and microscopic organisms.
