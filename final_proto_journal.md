# Final Prototype Reflective journal

## Final Prototype week 1

The time has come to work on out final prototypes! For this final project, I wondered if there was anything that I had wanted to make this semester that I did not get to make for whatever reason, and after reading my past journal entries I remembered that I had wanted to make a sort of mold growth simulation but had given up on the idea because we had not went over arrays yet. This is what inspired the idea of creating a kind of micro world simulation, as I have always loved coding things that have behaviors that simulate life in a way. I have made a very simple [ecosystem simulation](https://www.slnova.org/yogurtfairy/projects/934296/play/) in Starlogo Nova before, and a [virtual petri dish that reacts to light, tilting and shaking](https://github.com/yogurtdairy/CART253/blob/main/images/VID_20260402_173738.mp4) in Max msp, but I have never made anything of this kind before using JavaScript. I love biology (it was actually one of my strongest subjects in high school, I even got an excellence award), and despite not choosing to pursue this subject in my higher education, I still enjoy incorporating biological systems and themes into my work like natural ecosystems and microscopic organisms. 

My end goal for this prototype is to have an interactive microscopic world where the user can spawn multiple different kinds of bacteria or single celled organisms of sorts that have their own behaviors and can grow, eat, reproduce, die and interact with the others. I want this project to be very playful, where you can have fun with it, influence the behaviors of the organisms in whatever way you wish, and maybe possibly crash everything by spawning way to many things at once (invasive species simulator lol). 

This first week I was able to do some decent progress at home and in class. I currently have two species of microorganisms, smaller green ones (spawned with mouse pressed) that easily turn invasive, and red fat ones (spawned currently by pressing any key) that eat the green ones until they die of obesity. Despite not having that much biodiversity currently, I still think this project is already lots of fun to play around with. This was by complete accident, but if every single organism of either species dies, you cannot spawn any more of them and total species extinction has occurred. As such, I find it entertaining to just spend time trying to keep this fragile ecosystem alive, since it cannot be self sustained in its current state (I have not yet implemented reproduction). Once I implement a way for the organisms to reproduce, I believe it will be really cool if this ecosystem is originally self sustaining and the user can influence it and destabilize the natural order (or fix it!)

Next week, I plan to keep iterating and messing around with the two current species' behaviors and adding news ones like the aforementioned reproduction, and possibly if I have the time I wish to add a third species for even more diversity. I want to also keep working on the visual aspect of the project as I really wish to achieve the illusion that the weird colorful things on screen are alive despite obviously not actually being alive at all in the biological sense.
